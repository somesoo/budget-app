# Budget

A shared budget for one to four people, on everyone's phone. Adding an
expense is three taps (category → amount → Save), and every phone sees it
straight away.

- A plan per month, with a limit per category and money put aside first
- Savings accounts and goals, trips you spend from, IKE/IKZE/PPK
- Debts with a payoff plan (snowball or avalanche), and net worth over time
- Subscriptions and yearly bills, with what to put aside each month
- How much is really free to spend, the age of your money, the road to
  financial independence
- Charts over the last six months, and optional AI advice (Google Gemini)
- English and Polish; installs to the home screen like an app

## Your own copy in five minutes

You need a free [Vercel](https://vercel.com) and a free
[Supabase](https://supabase.com) account.

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Fsomesoo%2Fbudget-app&project-name=budget&repository-name=budget&stores=%5B%7B%22type%22%3A%22integration%22%2C%22integrationSlug%22%3A%22supabase%22%2C%22productSlug%22%3A%22supabase%22%7D%5D)

1. Click the button, sign in with GitHub.
2. When it asks for Supabase, create a new database (free plan, a region
   near you).
3. Deploy, then open the site: pick an email and password, type the names.
4. On each phone: open the site → *Add to Home Screen*.

Details, the manual way and troubleshooting: [docs/install.md](docs/install.md).
Updating: [docs/updating.md](docs/updating.md).
